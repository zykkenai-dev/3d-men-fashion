THE WAY IT ALL COMES TOGETHER

Three pieces.
Your rotation.

A leather jacket gives a soft knit its structure. A relaxed wool trouser brings a little polish. Start with these three, then make the combinations your own.

Layer with intention. Wear the Field Jacket open over the Essential Knit. The cropped jacket keeps the shape clear while the softer layer adds warmth.

Find the balance. Pair the relaxed Studio Trouser with the jacket for an easy silhouette, or wear it with just the knit for a quieter day.

Keep colour connected. Tobacco and Oat bring warmth to Charcoal. Ink with Stone feels sharper. Repeat one shade in your footwear to pull the look together.

KEEP THE GOOD THINGS

A little care.
A longer rotation.

Give your pieces time to air between wears. Clean with the material in mind, and always follow the garment label.

Leather: protect the finish

Wipe the Field Jacket with a soft, dry cloth. Keep it away from prolonged heat and direct sunlight, and store it on a broad hanger so the shoulders hold their shape. For cleaning, use a leather specialist.

Merino: keep the shape

Hand wash the Essential Knit in cold water with a gentle wool detergent. Avoid wringing. Reshape while damp and dry flat, then fold for storage. Hanging can stretch the shoulders and length.

Tailoring: let it breathe

Air the Studio Trouser between wears and steam gently to relax creases. Use a trouser hanger to keep the drape clean. Dry clean when needed rather than after every wear.
