PROPORTION BEFORE EVERYTHING

Find your fit.

Start with how you like to wear your clothes. Use the size guide on each piece to compare the demo measurements with a garment you already enjoy wearing.

The Field Jacket: leave room for a layer

Choose your usual size for the intended relaxed shape. Check the chest and body length in the size guide, and allow space for the knit underneath. The shorter body works well with trousers sitting naturally at the waist.

The Essential Knit: soft, easy volume

Your usual size gives an easy silhouette. For a closer shape, compare the chest measurement with a knit you own before sizing down. Wear it loose over the waistband or with a light front tuck to define the proportions.

The Studio Trouser: begin at the waist

Start with your usual waist size, then compare waist and inseam measurements in the guide. The front pleats and relaxed leg provide ease. Check the length with your chosen footwear for the amount of break you prefer.
